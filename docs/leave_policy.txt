Leave Policy

New employees receive 18 days of paid leave per year. Leave accrues monthly, at 1.5 days per month, starting from the first day of employment. Unused leave can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

Sick leave is separate from paid leave. Every employee gets 8 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

To request leave, submit the request in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Your manager must approve or decline within 2 working days.
