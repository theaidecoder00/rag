Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, agreed with their manager. Team anchor days, when everyone is in the office, are Tuesday and Thursday.

Employees working remotely must be available on chat during core hours, 11 AM to 4 PM local time. Company laptops must use the VPN when connecting to internal systems.

The company provides a one-time home office allowance of 15,000 rupees for a chair, monitor, or desk. Internet reimbursement is not provided.
